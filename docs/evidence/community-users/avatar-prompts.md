# Community user illustrated avatar prompts

The four avatars were regenerated individually with the built-in image generation tool, then resized to 512×512 PNG and assigned to the corresponding Forgejo accounts.

Shared direction:

> A polished character-illustration head-and-shoulders portrait for a fictional Japanese community user. Premium modern anime-inspired editorial illustration, clean linework, gentle cel shading, centered square composition, simple colored background, and a face that remains recognizable at 40px. No photo realism, robot, mascot, text, logo, border, or watermark.

Individual direction:

| Account | Character direction | Background |
|---|---|---|
| `haruka-sato` | Young adult woman, short dark-auburn bob, teal hair clip, cream hoodie, curious home-GPU hobbyist | Muted teal |
| `takumi-endo` | Young adult man, wavy navy-black hair, round glasses, charcoal overshirt, analytical CAD/SVG researcher | Cobalt blue |
| `nana-kurose` | Young adult woman, black side ponytail, amber earrings, green cardigan, cheerful Docker learner | Amber orange |
| `rio-kanda` | Young adult man, tousled brown hair with a copper streak, mustard knit, thoughtful reading-circle organizer | Violet purple |

Final project assets are stored under `seed/assets/user-avatars/`.
